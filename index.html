The Bahr Experience

Where confidence meets aquatic excellence.

BahrSwimmingClub is a Zanzibar-based aquatic sports and training organization dedicated to helping people learn, improve and experience the water with confidence.

From first-time swimmers to athletes preparing for competitive and open-water challenges, our approach combines technique, discipline, safety and enjoyment.

✓Professional swimming instruction for children and adults
✓Private and group coaching
✓Pool and open-water experiences
✓Performance, confidence and water safety
