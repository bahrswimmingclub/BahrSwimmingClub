01 · Our mission

Creating opportunity through the water.

BahrSwimmingClub Funds is an initiative of Bahr Swimming Club dedicated to expanding access to swimming, water-safety education, aquatic sports development and community programs in Zanzibar.

Through financial support, volunteer expertise, partnerships and community initiatives, we connect people with opportunities to learn, train, compete and become safer around water.
